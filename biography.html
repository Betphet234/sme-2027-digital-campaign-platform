My Biography

Saintmoses Eromosele - SME

Businessman, administrator, author, education advocate, community development practitioner and humanitarian.

Background and education

Saintmoses Eromosele, popularly known as SME, is from Ewu in Esan Central Local Government Area of Edo State. He studied Law at Ambrose Alli University, Ekpoma, Economics at Benson Idahosa University, and Sociology and Anthropology at the University of Benin.

His additional training includes Procurement Management at the University of Benin, Leadership and Management courses from the University of Washington, German language studies at Berlitz and other institutions in Germany, and European human-rights studies at the European University Viadrina in Germany and Poland.

Professional and community life

SME is the Chief Executive Officer of GIVITEC LTD and GIVITEC Properties. He is also involved in the administration of Benin Medical Centre and Patriots Demonstration School.

He has served in different community, educational, cultural and humanitarian leadership positions, including Secretary-General of the Esan Descendants Assembly, Pioneer National President of the Esan Students Association of Nigeria, Pioneer National President of the Ewu Students Association of Nigeria, Executive Director of Oneghe Sele Foundation, and Co-founder of the Ewu Progress Foundation.

Author and advocate

His works include The Winds of Life, The Trials of an Empire, 48 Laws of Leadership, Silent Night, The Boy Who Sang in Silence, and History and Chronicle of Ewu; Since 1440, among others.
